HC3 heteroskedasticity-consistent standard errors and residual-df t/F inference were used. The genre F test jointly tests the six genre coefficients. Panel B reports M1 length-adjusted Rap contrasts; estimates are Rap minus the comparison genre. Bonferroni adjustment is across the six Rap-versus-other-genre contrasts separately for each outcome. The 95% CIs and unadjusted p values are pointwise; only the final p column is Bonferroni-adjusted. These analyses assess robustness to line length and should not be interpreted as causal mediation. Em dashes indicate that line length was not included.

Diagnostic interaction. A separate genre × average line-length model indicated variation in the MDD–line-length relationship across genres, F(6, 547) = 3.702, p = .001. No corresponding interaction was detected for MHD, F(6, 547) = 0.646, p = .694. These interaction models were diagnostic and were not used to replace the prespecified M1 analyses reported above.
